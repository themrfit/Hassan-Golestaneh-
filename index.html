About Me

The Story of a Pioneer

Born on March 21, 1983 in Tehran, Iran, Hassan Golestaneh is recognized as the founder of competitive fitness in Iran and the first Iranian athlete to win world championship medals in fitness.

He began his athletic journey with football under legendary coaches Mohammad Panjali and Nasser Hejazi. After an ankle injury forced him to retire from football at age 19, he practiced jujitsu for seven years and won multiple medals before fully dedicating himself to bodybuilding and fitness.

From 2009 to 2015 he coached the Iranian national team in international fitness competitions, earning the title of World’s Best Fitness Coach in 2015. In 2016 he stepped onto the stage himself and made history.

WFF World Championships – Silver Medal

Became the first Iranian athlete to win a world medal in fitness. Secured the silver medal (runner-up) in the Men’s Fitness Model division, making history for Iranian sports.

Won the gold medal at the World Fitness Championships in the USA. Also ranked among the Top 10 models at the Arnold Classic — the first Iranian athlete to compete in the United States during the travel ban period.
